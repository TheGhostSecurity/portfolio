01 / Land technology

ArdhiLens

Mobile land verification & risk assessment

ArdhiLens explores how a mobile service can make land verification more transparent and reliable. Designed around a Kinondoni Municipal Council case study, it helps buyers and sellers verify ownership, identity, and location while assessing potential transaction risks.

  • Buyer and seller registration with secure authentication
  • Land, seller identity, and GPS-based location verification
  • Rule-based fraud risk assessment and notifications
  • Digital verification certificate generation
